Padilla Jr.

Software, data, and manufacturing engineer based in Mountain View, California.

I build software and data systems for real-world physical systems. My background spans industrial software, manufacturing systems, machine learning, biomedical engineering, automation, testing, and engineering analytics.

I earned a B.S. in Biomedical Engineering with a minor in Computer Science from San Jose State University. That combination shaped how I work: understand the physical process, make the data trustworthy, and build software that helps people operate the system more effectively.

Most recently, I worked at D-Tek Manufacturing as a Manufacturing Engineer focused on quality, data integrity, manufacturing systems, automation, and process improvement. My work included Python automation, Power BI dashboards, ERP/MRP production data, MES analytics, NPI/NPD support, traceability, root-cause analysis, production optimization, equipment troubleshooting, and controlled documentation.

That work reduced quality misses by approximately 50% and improved on-time delivery by approximately 30%. Across earlier roles at Cellares, Jabil, AbbVie, iSono Health, Stanford, and SJSU, I worked on production data, machine-learning-assisted inspection, scientific automation, medical-device software, autonomous-vehicle sensing, and robotic instrumentation.
